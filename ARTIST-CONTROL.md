# Artist control, participation, and living terms

**Working draft, version 0.11 — 15 November 2026**

## 1. The essential term: I remain in control of myself

**I retain full control over my body, my boundaries, my participation, and the creative direction of this artwork.** A purchase gives nobody authority over my person or an entitlement to an interaction I have not freely agreed to. Each participant retains the same control over themselves.

I can decline an activity, pause, or end my participation. The participant can do the same. Any resulting service, cancellation, or refund question must be handled under the agreed booking and applicable rights; nobody’s bodily autonomy depends on a financial settlement.

## 2. Participation in a conceptual performative artwork

The subject is a conceptual performative artwork. Its material can include conversation, presence, improvisation, evolving text, precise ambiguity, and the ceremonial machinery of an ultra contract. The work is not required to have one fixed artistic meaning.

Before participating, each person must understand and affirmatively agree to the identified version and actual scope. An acknowledgement can read:

> I understand that I am choosing to participate in an evolving conceptual performative artwork. I agree to the identified terms and session scope, including the artist’s creative control and the change process below. I retain my own boundaries and can decline, pause, or stop an interaction.

This acknowledgement does not enlarge a touch permission or replace the concrete [service specification](SERVICE.md).

## 3. Changeability is itself part of the terms

**The artwork and its published draft terms are changeable. That changeability is an express part of this agreement.** The artist may develop the public work and propose new versions. Each published revision must carry a version and date. That creative freedom and the terms of an existing purchase operate as follows:

| Change | How it operates |
| --- | --- |
| Public draft, future offer, or artistic commentary | The artist may revise it. Publishing a new version does not itself replace terms already accepted for a purchase. |
| Improvisation within the agreed performance scope | The artist retains creative direction within that scope. Boundaries and ongoing consent still apply. |
| Material change to an existing booking | Explain the proposed change, provide the new version, and obtain affirmative agreement before applying it. This includes changes to price, quantity, duration, session format, refund arrangements, or permissions. |
| New or expanded bodily contact | Obtain the relevant person’s specific, revocable permission under the consent protocol. An agreement to evolving terms is not advance consent to all future contact. |
| Immediate pause or withdrawal | Either person can stop an interaction immediately. A pause does not create a new fee, waiver, or revised booking by itself. |

Silence, continued website use, an earlier payment, or the phrase “terms may change” does not supply agreement to a material booking change. Keep the accepted version and record any agreed amendment. A person may decline proposed new terms without a penalty for that refusal.

If the original booking cannot continue and no replacement is agreed, address the remaining paid commitment under the agreed cancellation/refund arrangements and applicable rights. Publishing different terms does not erase an existing obligation.

## 4. Craziness as artistic persona

The artist’s self-described **“craziness”**, including play with jurisdiction-dependent labels, forms part of the brand’s absurdity and performance. This is an artistic self-description. It does not represent a medical diagnosis, a court finding about legal capacity, or immunity from responsibility.

## 5. The absurdity paradox and actual disputes

The artist invokes the [Independent Tribunal’s absurdity-paradox research](https://independenttribunal.org/research/absurdity-paradox/) as context for the collision between an intentionally absurd artwork and expectations of literal meaning. The linked article examines a hypothetical insult through defamation and criminal communications law. It does not establish a special procedure for disputes about this artwork.

The artwork’s [Independent Tribunal](TRIBUNAL.md) may discuss that paradox within its fictional framework. Actual complaints and legal claims remain subject to the practical dispute arrangements and applicable law. These terms subject no person or their family to a threat, sexual-violence-themed condition, retaliation, or penalty for raising a concern or seeking a remedy. Seeking a remedy does not by itself enrol anyone in a tribunal performance or grant permission to publish their identity.

Where UK consumer law applies, the [CMA’s guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/writing-a-fair-contract-for-customers) cautions against variation terms that give a trader a blank cheque. The [Consumer Rights Act’s indicative list](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/15/schedule/2?view=plain), particularly paragraphs 11 and 20, addresses unilateral changes and hindering legal remedies. These references do not select the governing law for a particular booking.

**Consensually:** the artist controls their own participation and creative choices; each participant controls their own agreement, boundaries, and decision to continue.
