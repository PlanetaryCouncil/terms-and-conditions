# Iceland Eclipse minimal disclaimer

**Working draft, version 0.8 — 14 November 2026**

*The artwork’s short session disclaimer, adapted from the supplied visual reference. Read alongside the [service specification](SERVICE.md) and [consent protocol](CONSENT.md).*

This disclaimer accompanies the artwork’s Ultra Magic Session offering, presented in the artistic frame of [Ultra Magic Hands](https://www.ultramagichands.com/). The selected activity and its boundaries are confirmed before each session.

## Precise ambiguity

**We engineer this agreement with precise ambiguity. Feature, not a bug.**

Interpretation, atmosphere, and the artwork’s evolving meaning remain open. The minimum service, price agreed before payment, scope of touch, and right to stop must be understandable to everyone involved. Ambiguity does not supply a missing agreement.

**Maximize the luck surface.** Chance and serendipity are part of the artistic frame; no particular personal outcome is promised by the session package.

**We will decide later.** Coin-flip regulation, humidity measurement, trial by combat, and the Mecha-Godzilla Clause are the agreement’s [priority schedules](REGULATIONS.md). Their detailed procedures remain reserved.

## Session acknowledgement

- [ ] I understand the agreed session format, activities, duration, and any relevant setting-specific risks explained before we begin.
- [ ] For remote delivery, I understand that the artist will be present live on Zoom. I have agreed whether the session is business consultancy, Reiki, self-guided participation, or a specifically described massage-related activity; these formats are not interchangeable without my agreement.
- [ ] I understand that wellbeing, relaxation, emotional release, or other personal effects may vary. No particular outcome is guaranteed. The agreed service still has to be delivered.
- [ ] I have had an opportunity to share relevant boundaries, access needs, allergies, or restrictions that I want accommodated. I do not have to provide a full medical history. This acknowledgement is not medical clearance.
- [ ] I choose the session voluntarily. Any bodily contact requires my separate, specific, revocable permission under the consent protocol.
- [ ] I can pause, decline an activity, or stop at any time. I am invited to communicate discomfort when able; the provider must also notice and respond to signs of distress or withdrawal.
- [ ] I understand that session participation does not authorise recording, publication, or sexual contact, and does not waive rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

**Before starting, we confirm the session scope and the chosen consent mode. Continuing alone does not establish consent, fitness for an activity, or permission to expand the scope.**

The [Independent Tribunal](TRIBUNAL.md) and its review layers are artistic roles performed by the artist. Their fictional declaration of independence does not remove the artist’s actual interest or replace the practical complaints route.
