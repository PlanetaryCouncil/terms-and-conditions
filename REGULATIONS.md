# The important regulations

**Working draft, version 0.9 — 15 November 2026**

**We will decide later.**

Coin-flip regulation. Humidity measurement. Trial by combat. The Mecha-Godzilla Clause. These are the priority subjects for the next development of the artwork’s agreement. Their prominence is settled; their detailed machinery is not.

**A very ultra contract. We have all the nuances. Some are specifically reserved for later.** Its conflict regulation sits in the [Independent Tribunal framework](TRIBUNAL.md#3-conflict-regulation-and-internal-defaults), including the artist’s disclosed interest and multiple artistic roles. Its humidity and moisture questions sit in Schedule B. There is an entire Mecha-Godzilla Clause awaiting its own definition in Schedule D.

**We engineer this agreement with precise ambiguity. Feature, not a bug.** The following schedules reserve the questions that matter without pretending that their answers have already been agreed.

**Couldism, wouldism, shouldism will prevail.** This is the conditional language of the artwork and its unfinished machinery. It keeps possibilities available while decisions remain reserved; it does not create consent or rewrite an existing agreement.

## Schedule A — Coin-flip regulation

**Status: to be decided later.**

Reserved questions include the eligible coin, allocation of its faces, the person conducting the flip, witnesses, permitted interference (if any), edge landings, repeat flips, and the flip that decides which flipping procedure applies.

A coin flip without a coin-flip regulation presents an obvious regulatory difficulty. Whether that difficulty requires a preliminary coin flip is itself reserved.

## Schedule B — Humidity measurement

**Status: to be decided later.**

Reserved questions include relative or absolute humidity, units, instruments, calibration, the Humidity Measurement Officer, measurement location and timing, acceptable conditions, conflicting readings, and the relationship between the reading and the validity of a flip.

“Moisture” is also part of the artwork’s vocabulary. The precise quantity to be measured, its definition, and the role of the reading remain reserved along with the rest of this schedule.

No numerical threshold or measurement standard is adopted in this draft. A number will not become authoritative merely because it has appeared in a previous artwork. The humidity must await its regulation, and the regulation must account for the humidity.

## Schedule C — Trial by combat

**Status: ceremonial procedure to be decided later.**

Reserved questions include election of the procedure, notice, the non-contact form of the ordeal, observers, conversion to a coin flip, and referral to the Independent Tribunal.

“Trial by combat” is retained as the title of a proposed artistic procedure. This draft authorises no physical fight, application of force, dangerous venue, or compulsory ordeal. No contest is scheduled by mentioning it.

## Schedule D — The Mecha-Godzilla Clause

**Status: deliberately undrafted.**

The Mecha-Godzilla Clause is important enough not to be settled merely to complete the document. Its content, function, activation conditions, and relationship to every other schedule remain reserved.

The clause will be decided later. Deciding what the clause decides may require a further decision. That decision is also reserved.

## The eventual deciding figure

Within the artwork, questions about this machinery ultimately return to the artist acting as the [Independent Tribunal](TRIBUNAL.md). The intervening layers and the relationship between the four schedules remain proposals for later development. The Tribunal’s circular declaration of independence remains part of the performance.

## Effect of an unresolved schedule

“To be decided later” means no operative procedure has yet been agreed. It does not incorporate another document automatically, activate a default coin flip, or authorise the artist to impose new booking terms retrospectively. Future operative terms require an identified version and the relevant parties’ agreement.

These schedules concern the artwork’s ceremonial machinery. They do not supply bodily consent, change the service already purchased, or displace the practical complaints route. Existing [consent rules](CONSENT.md) and [session commitments](SERVICE.md) remain explicit while the regulatory artwork develops.
