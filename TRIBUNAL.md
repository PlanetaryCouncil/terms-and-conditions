# Independent Tribunal and the layers of interpretation

**Working draft, version 0.5 — 14 November 2026**

**Artistic framework and satire.** The offices below are roles performed by the artist. Their titles do not represent external institutions or independent adjudicators. The practical dispute arrangements are stated separately below.

## 1. The layers

| Layer | Office | Function | Officeholder |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The Encounter | Establish what was said, offered, and experienced. | The artist, hearing the participant’s account. |
| 2 | Ultra Magic Session Review | Consider which agreed session format and defaults describe the encounter. | The artist in the capacity of session reviewer. |
| 3 | Office of Precise Ambiguity | Examine competing interpretations and determine whether the ambiguity is sufficiently precise. | The artist in an interpretive capacity. |
| 4 | Independent Tribunal | Determine questions arising within the artwork’s internal framework. | The artist in the capacity of the independent figure. |
| 5 | Chamber for the Independence of the Independent Tribunal | Review the independence of Layer 4 and the independence of this review. | The same artist in a further independent capacity. |

**Ultimately, the independent figure is me.** Each layer may refer an artistic question to the next. Layer 5 is the final internal layer and refers its conclusion to the artist, who is already present. Changing the office does not change the person. That is the architecture.

## 2. Declaration of absolute non-conflict

*The following is the Tribunal’s fictional, self-certifying declaration:*

> There is absolutely no conflict of interest. Even if there is a conflict of interest, this conflict of interest is not a conflict of interest. Whether a conflict of interest is a conflict of interest shall be determined by the Independent Tribunal. The Independent Tribunal is me. Any appeal concerning my independence shall be heard by me in an independently designated capacity. The designation is independent because I have independently designated it.

The declaration is the artwork’s circular proposition. It does not establish actual impartiality. **The artist is also the provider and has a direct interest in the agreement.** That interest is disclosed, not erased by changing a title or repeating a declaration.

## 3. Internal defaults

1. Questions of artistic interpretation ultimately return to the artist acting as the Independent Tribunal.
2. If an office has no separately appointed officeholder, the artist performs the role. No separate person is implied.
3. An appeal adds a layer of interpretation; it does not create a new independent reviewer.
4. The Tribunal may give the artwork a final internal interpretation. It cannot retrospectively create consent, alter the purchased service, certify delivery that did not occur, or decide another person’s private wishes.
5. There is no fee, financial sanction, public-shaming obligation, or compulsory ordeal for questioning a fictional determination or declining the tribunal performance.
6. No physical combat, coin-flip dispute procedure, monetary obligation, or third-party jurisdiction from the reference PDFs is incorporated by this document.

## 4. Practical complaints and disputes

A participant may raise a service complaint directly with the provider using the contact details in the booking. A complaint does not have to pass through the artistic layers. Participation in the tribunal performance is optional and does not delay or condition any applicable deadline or remedy.

If the parties want mediation or another independent review, they may jointly choose a person who is separate from the artist and disclose relevant interests before agreeing to that process. This draft does not impose compulsory arbitration, make the artist the final legal judge of their own dispute, or restrict access to competent courts, regulators, or applicable complaint mechanisms.

No governing law, court, or binding arbitration agreement is selected here. See the [choice-of-law note](CHOICE-OF-LAW.md). The artist’s internal determination does not override the [service specification](SERVICE.md), [consent protocol](CONSENT.md), or rights under applicable law.

As a legal drafting reference, where UK consumer law applies, the [CMA’s unfair-contract-terms guidance](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a609329b00f3323bf1a23f3/unfair_contract_terms_guidance.pdf), section on dispute resolution and final decision terms, addresses concerns about trader-controlled determinations and the role of independent adjudication. This reference does not assert that UK law governs every session.
